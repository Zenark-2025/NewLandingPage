## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a serene, hyper-refined sanctuary designed for conscious mental wellness through empathetic artificial intelligence. Striking an equilibrium between bespoke editorial luxury and tactile hardware-grade precision, the interface projects clarity, warmth, and grounded presence. The aesthetic takes inspiration from modern haute horlogerie, contemporary wellness retreats, and top-tier consumer technology hardware. 

The visual design language integrates **Glassmorphism** with subtle **Physical/Tactile** qualities: gossamer champagne layers, warm satin backdrops, and luminous violet lighting. Micro-interactions should feel weighted and deliberate, emulating physical drag, optical fluid expansion, and natural breathing intervals.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture is constructed upon warm, tactile neutrals instead of clinical grays, grounding the user in comfort and calm:

- **Primary (`#5C4E99`)**: Introspective Violet. Imparts grounded wisdom, focus, and intelligent clarity. Reserved for primary actions, critical state indicators, and brand anchoring.
- **Secondary (`#C6A875`)**: Satin Gold. Brings quiet nobility and human craft. Used for luxury detailing, badges, delicate linear framing, and completion states.
- **Tertiary (`#A99BD6`)**: Lilac Glow. Represents reactive ambient feedback, soft AI consciousness, volumetric shadows, and illumination fields.
- **Neutral (`#FAF7F0`)**: Warm Satin. The primary canvas substrate, avoiding sterile glare and offering an organic, paper-like warmth.
- **Deep Aubergine (`#2B2350`)**: Reserved for authoritative typography, high-impact sections, and deep contemplative finales.
- **Champagne Panel (`#F3EDE1`)**: Used for nested cards, interactive tiers, and subtle depth separations.

## Typography

Typography balances evocative literary elegance with surgical technological accuracy:

- **Editorial Serifs (Playfair Display)**: Dictate emotional atmosphere, reflective meditations, and architectural section headlines. Rendered with natural proportional rhythm and slightly tightened tracking.
- **Precision Sans (Inter)**: Handles conversational outputs, behavioral feedback, form interfaces, and metadata. Ensures clinical legibility at all device sizes.
- **Labeling Details**: All micro-labels, eyebrow tags, and status tokens use uppercase Inter with generous tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mimic crafted horological or architectural typography.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural philosophy relies on an intentional, quiet rhythm with generous negative space that induces lower cognitive load:

- **Grid Architecture**: A disciplined 12-column desktop grid with a maximum content container of `1280px`. Mobile collapses into a fluid single-column stack with structured 4-column sub-alignments.
- **Whitespace Cadence**: Outer section margins are expansive, allowing conversational AI prompts and reflections to breathe as single focal entities. 
- **Reflow Rules**: Multi-column comparisons and biometric tracking widgets shift from multi-axis grids on desktop to linear horizontally scrolling cards or layered stacks on smaller viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial hierarchy abandons artificial high-contrast dropshadows in favor of ethereal illumination and physical material layering:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base layer sits at Warm Satin (`#FAF7F0`). Level 1 elevation uses Champagne Panel (`#F3EDE1`) with a 0.5px border of `rgba(198, 168, 117, 0.25)`. Level 2 floating layers utilize Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) backed with soft environmental light.
- **Luminescent Diffusion**: Instead of dark drop shadows, floating entities project an ambient aura tinted with Tertiary Lilac (`rgba(169, 155, 214, 0.12)`) and warm undertones (`rgba(43, 35, 80, 0.04)`), featuring an extended blur radius (32px to 48px).
- **Subsurface Glow**: Active interactive zones, such as the active AI reflection prompt or input field, emit a radial inner and outer gradient pulse of `#A99BD6` simulating living warmth and mindful breathing.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a tailored organic radius (`roundedness: 2` with standard radii scaling to `1rem` and `1.5rem`). Corners avoid severe sharpness or uncrafted pill extremes, offering pebble-like geometry that invites gentle human touch without sacrificing structural discipline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Filled Introspective Violet (`#5C4E99`) with crisp `#FFFFFF` text. Micro-beveled top highlight with `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` and a soft 16px ambient purple shadow. Hover state smoothly transitions through a gold-infused lilac boundary.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Pure White or Champagne background with a hairline Satin Gold border (`#C6A875`, 1px) and `#2B2350` text.
- **Interactive State Transitions**: 300ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) transition timing across all hover and press states.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed from Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Champagne (`#F3EDE1`) resting on the Warm Satin background. 
- Finished with 1px border colored `rgba(198, 168, 117, 0.2)`. 
- Padding scales from `1.5rem` on mobile to `2.5rem` on desktop to preserve serene negative space.

### Input Fields & Conversational Area
- Input fields use soft champagne inset styling (`background: rgba(243, 237, 225, 0.6)`) with subtle inward inset shadowing.
- Focus state brings an organic Lilac Glow outline ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(169, 155, 214, 0.35)`) and seamless transition to pure white background fill.
- Placeholder text is styled in muted tone (`#6E697F`).

### Chips & Selection Pills
- Compact, pill-shaped tags used for emotional states, tags, and cognitive themes.
- Unselected: Semi-transparent champagne with subtle gold stroke.
- Selected: Deep Aubergine fill (`#2B2350`) with satin gold text (`#D9C7A8`) and soft haptic scale feedback.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Custom sculpted circular selectors with 1.5px satin gold frames. 
- Selected state activates an inward blooming disc in `#5C4E99` accompanied by an ultra-soft `#A99BD6` blur ring.

### Mindful Breath & AI Pulse (Domain Component)
- A non-invasive breathing indicator that visually contracts and expands on an 8-second cycle using an organic radial gradient from `#FAF7F0` to `#A99BD6`, grounding the user during AI responses.
